VERDICT: HOLDS (part 1; Hermitian is NOT needed if kappa is the complex-bilinear form)

STATEMENT TESTED: det X = (tr X)^2/4 + (1/8) kappa(iX0, iX0), X0 = X - (tr X/2) 1, kappa(A,B) = tr(ad_A ad_B). ad_A is the 3x3 matrix whose columns are the coordinates of [A, E_k] in the basis E_j = i s_j (commutators computed per sample, no shortcut formula). Measured: kappa(E_j, E_k) = -8 delta_jk.

METHOD: mpmath at 50 digits, own 2x2 complex arithmetic, fixed seed. 10,000 random inputs per class; residual reported relative to the largest of the three terms. Negative controls with wrong constants confirm the test can fail.

EVIDENCE: `python B1_check.py` (16 s). Max relative residual:

SCOPE: Holds to working precision in every class, including indefinite, negative, singular, zero, and extreme scales. It also holds for arbitrary complex 2x2 X provided kappa is the complex-bilinear Killing form of sl(2,C). Hermitian is needed only for iX0 to lie in real su(2) (coordinates real: imaginary parts exactly 0 in (a)-(d), order 1 in (e)) and for the terms to be real.
